Question

(photo) Choose the statement that best describes the picture.

Choices

  1. (A) A presenter is pointing at a slide on a screen.
  2. (B) A security guard is standing at a gate.
  3. (C) A chef is baking bread in an oven.
  4. (D) A woman is pushing a stroller down a sidewalk.
Correct answer: A
Explanation: Option A correctly shows a presenter pointing at a slide projected on a screen in a meeting room. The other options describe different roles and locations.
