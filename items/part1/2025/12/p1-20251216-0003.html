Question

(photo) Choose the statement that best describes the picture.

Choices

  1. (A) A musician is performing in a subway tunnel.
  2. (B) A chef is washing vegetables in a sink.
  3. (C) A nurse is arranging medicine on a cart.
  4. (D) People are standing in front of ticket machines at a station.
Correct answer: D
Explanation: Option D correctly shows travelers standing in front of ticket machines at a railway station. The other options describe unrelated jobs and locations.
