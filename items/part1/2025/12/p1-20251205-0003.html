Question

(photo) Choose the statement that best describes the picture.

Choices

  1. (A) A cashier is handing a receipt to a customer.
  2. (B) A driver is filling a car with gasoline.
  3. (C) A woman is folding clothes on a sofa.
  4. (D) A family is having a meal together at a kitchen table.
Correct answer: D
Explanation: The picture shows family members eating at a table in a kitchen. The other options show different activities.
